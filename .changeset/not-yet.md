`NOT_YET` — what BYOLLM does not do yet, as one list: tool use, access to your
local files, streaming, job kinds beyond the ones `JobKind` defines (derived
from it, not typed), and jobs while your device is offline. With it,
`NOT_YET_LEDE`, `NOT_YET_TAIL`, `notYetSentence()` for the paragraph and
`notYetLine()` for a list entry, so every surface renders the same words. The
README's Status and byo-llm.com now list it, and a test fails when either
copy disagrees with the export.
