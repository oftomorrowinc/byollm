# 0.1.4

**One sentence changes: the hub-fence now says what the hub sees. And `byollm status` names the brake when it refuses a job for someone else.**

`PROTOCOL_VERSION` is still `2`. A daemon on 0.1.4 talks to a hub or relay on
0.1.0 through 0.1.3, and the other way round. The wire and the on-disk shapes
are unchanged, and there is no migration to apply. Upgrade when it suits you.

## What changed

**The hub-fence sentence, reworded.** `HUB_FENCE` in `@byollm/protocol` — the
one sentence the hub, the relay, the docs and byollm.cloud's privacy page all
assert verbatim — now reads:

> byollm.cloud sees who asked, which devices and sites are connected, which
> device did each job, and each job's kind, size, outcome and time. Never
> prompts, never answers, never credentials.

It used to open "sees only what routing and metering need" and then list
fields. Three times a new fact the hub kept failed that opening and needed a
ruling, so the justification is gone and the sentence states what is seen.
What makes it checkable is unchanged and still shipped: `HUB_FENCE_CLAUSES`
maps every column the hub keeps to a clause of the sentence — who asked;
devices and sites, which now claims the device that served each job (one row
per person and device, not a row per job); kind, size and outcome; time; and
the identifiers that are not facts about anyone. A column that fits no clause
is a decision, not a quiet addition. The hub and the web pin this version and
their fence tests change in the same release.

**`byollm status` names the brake.** A device that runs work for other people
refuses it when a community budget trips — jobs per hour, jobs per day,
payload size, or a ledger it cannot read — and until now status showed the
counts and nothing else. Now each brake that fired in the last hour prints one
line with its count, its limit and the fix:

```
refused 21 in the last hour: hourly cap (20) — raise it: byollm budget community --per-hour N
```

**`byollm budget community`.** The count caps were only in
`~/.byollm/config.json`. `byollm budget community [--per-hour N] [--per-day N]
[--count-team-jobs yes|no]` writes them, and ends by saying how the running
daemon picks them up (its supervisor is told, or `byollm stop && byollm
start`). `byollm offer` now says the same thing at its end.

**A teammate's metered job is not a stranger's.** A job from someone the hub
admits to your team, on a metered service you offered to the team under a
spend ceiling, no longer counts against the community job-count caps — your
ceiling already covers it. `community.countTeamJobs` (default `false`) turns
that back on. Payload, wall-clock and output caps still apply to every job,
and work that is not team-scoped is still counted.

**Live canaries tell a provider's refusal from a failure.** Under plain
`verify`, a control run that met the provider's quota or sign-in refusal
skips loudly and leaves a marker; under the release gate
(`BYOLLM_RELEASE_GATE=1`) it fails, and `tag.sh` refuses while the marker
exists. A model that answers without the canary fails in both modes.

**Said plainly, in two places.** byo-llm.com and the README now say where the
Gemini subscription stands beside the subscriptions byollm ships (not in yet;
no isolation for it). And byo-llm.com shows BYOLLM's own build log — the
newest five entries from the public feed, with the live line while something
is being built — linking to the whole log.
