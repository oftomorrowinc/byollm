# 0.1.5

**Site tools, images, and two fixes.**

`PROTOCOL_VERSION` is still `2`. **Update your hub or server first.** A
daemon on 0.1.5 works with a 0.1.4 upstream until you turn on one of the new
kinds, and if the upstream is older, it tells you and keeps serving
everything else. A 0.1.4 daemon works with a 0.1.5 hub and never sees the
new kinds. **There are four migrations** if you run the Supabase adapter:
two for the private Storage bucket that holds images and the table and
grants beside it (`20261104050000_blob_bucket`,
`20261104160000_blob_table_grants`), and two for site-tool calls in flight
and the site's count of them (`20261103230000_tool_calls`,
`20261104010000_tool_charges`). If you wrote your own store adapter, it
needs `putBlob`, `getBlob`, `deleteBlob` and the tool-call methods
(`recordToolCall`, `outstandingToolCalls`, `answerToolCall`, `toolResults`
and `chargeToolCall`).

## What changed

**Site tools.** A new kind, `llm.tools`, lets the model on your device call
tools a site declares, such as its search or its catalogue. The tools run on
the site's servers, never on your device: your device sends the model's
requests to that site, sealed, and hands back what the site returns. You see
the site's tools when you choose a model for them, and again if the site
changes them. It works on local and HTTP models; your Claude and Codex
subscriptions can't serve it yet. It is off until you turn it on for a
service. Every request is in `byollm log`. (byollm_012)

**Images.** `llm.vision` reads images and `llm.image` makes them, on local
and HTTP models, up to 20 MiB an image and eight to a job. Images travel by
reference, encrypted with a key only the job's two ends hold, and are
deleted once the site has its output. A teammate's images are budgeted
before your device downloads them (`community.maxImageBytes`, 16 MiB a job
by default). (byollm_021)

**The mapping claims the pair row's two times.** `HUB_FENCE_CLAUSES`' "and
time" clause now covers `first_at` and `last_at`, the two timestamps of the
hub's one-row-per-person-and-device record of which device last served whom
(#4058, #4131). They fit the clause, so this is a release and not a ruling.
`HUB_FENCE` — the sentence — is unchanged.

**A teammate's large job is admitted when it could fit.** Before fetching
another person's job, the daemon charged its size class's ceiling against
`community.maxPayloadChars`, and `large`'s ceiling is unbounded, so every declared-large job was refused at any limit you could set
(#5738). Admission now charges the class's floor — `sizeClassFloor`, new in
`@byollm/protocol`: small 0, medium 4,001, large 64,001 — and refuses only
what cannot fit; the opened payload is still measured against your limit
before any backend runs. Streamed (`unbounded`) community jobs are still
refused, now in words rather than "Infinity" (#5743).
